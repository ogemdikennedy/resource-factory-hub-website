01 / ABOUT RFH

Seeing potential where others see waste.

Resource Factory Hub Limited is a resource intelligence and recovery company exploring how materials can be better understood, utilised and transformed.

Our work combines practical experimentation with material documentation, circular product development and applied environmental research. We aim to make resource decisions more informed, measurable and useful.

Our approach

Understand. Investigate the resource challenge and available materials.

Analyse. Record material quantities, processes and uncertainties.

Develop. Explore practical recovery and circular product possibilities.

Document. Share evidence, limitations and lessons learned.

Exploratory research

Consumer Product Analysis & Greenwashing Assessment

Analysis of consumer products, ingredients, product information and sustainability-related claims to investigate potential greenwashing. This work grew from the founder's participation in a conference discussion about AI and greenwashing.

The associated convenience-sample survey involved 45 respondents. Recorded figures of 93.3%, 95.6% and 86.7% will be published with their precise question wording after final verification.

Developing research initiative

GreenMirror & Structured Product Data

Exploratory AI-assisted greenwashing assessment and a structured dataset covering 32 consumer products and 36 variables. GreenMirror is a developing initiative, not a deployed commercial detection service.
